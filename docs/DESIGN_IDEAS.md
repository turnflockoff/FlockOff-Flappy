# Design ideas (all street-legal)

Rule for every item: nothing may cover, tint, obscure or sit over any plate character, the state name or the registration sticker.

## 1. FLOCK OFF plate frame variants
| Variant | Look | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Circuit | Black frame, binary-code border, corner QR blocks, camera-eye at top-center, "TURNFLOCKOFF.COM — PRIVACY MATTERS" along the bottom | Current hero design |
| Dove | Matte white frame, pixel-art dove (from the game) in the top-center, red "FLOCK OFF" below | Ties to the game mascot |
| Minimal | Matte black, single-line "FLOCK OFF" bottom, thin red rule | Lowest cost to produce |
| Leaf border | Dark green frame with an engraved leaf vine running along the outer edge and corners only | Leaves are decoration around the plate, never over it |
| Eye-slash | Black frame with an eye-with-slash icon on each side, text at the bottom | Matches the "Cyberpunk Protest" frame already on the site |
Fit check: every variant needs a flat template overlay at standard US plate size (6" x 12") confirming the cut-out leaves all characters and the sticker areas clear.

## 2. Incognito Dove Disguise Glasses
- Packaging: window box showing the glasses; dove in a Groucho disguise on the front; tagline "You shouldn't need a disguise to run errands."
- Back-of-pack copy: "Novelty item. Costume use only — not a privacy device."
- Display: peg-hook card for retail; white-background product photo plus a mascot hero shot (see ARTWORK_PROMPTS.md).
- Unbranded temple arms (no third-party logos).

## 3. Stickers
- Pack of 5: (a) FLOCK OFF in red block letters, (b) camera with a slash, (c) pixel dove in a disguise, (d) "PRIVACY MATTERS" badge, (e) leaf-and-eye emblem.
- Palette: black #0e1116, red #ff3b3b, green #26d07c, off-white #e8edf2.
- Format: die-cut vinyl, 3" wide; printed note "Not for use on license plates."

## 4. Apparel
- Tee 1: pixel dove in disguise, "FLOCK OFF" underneath.
- Tee 2: camera-eye icon with "PRIVACY MATTERS."
- Tee 3: leaf-and-eye emblem small on the chest, TURNFLOCKOFF.COM on the back neck.
- Slogans: "Wave at the camera. Then vote." / "Your plate is not a public diary." / "Flock off."

## 5. Leaf-themed decorative art (never over a plate)
- Sticker and decal set: leaf-and-eye emblem, leaf vine corner flourishes for laptops and bumper-side panels.
- Leaf-border plate frame (variant above).
- Poster: autumn-leaf pattern forming a camera lens, caption "Nature doesn't need a camera."
- Enamel pin: green leaf with a small red eye.
